# Secret Heart — Our Unwritten Chapter V6

This version replaces the previous Secret Heart proposal structure with one continuous visual story.

## Flow
1. Minimal sealed-envelope password screen
2. Envelope/wax-seal opening animation after the correct key
3. Agnes portrait scene
4. "What my heart calls you" words around her portrait
5. Printed-photo table of memories already made
6. Empty Polaroids: memories not made yet
7. Minimal line-art "our little world" scene
8. Quiet hand-holding / last-breath scene
9. "Your choice" emotional pause
10. Proposal with the empty "Our First Future Memory" frame
11. Respectful "I need time" branch
12. Quiet YES reveal (no instant noisy celebration)
13. Four meaningful promises
14. Final keepsake: "all the memories we have not made yet"
