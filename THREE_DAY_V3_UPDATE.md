# Three Little Days — V3 Update

The three-day surprise overlay has been redesigned as a premium, light-colour, single-screen experience.

## Improvements

- Full overlay fits inside `100dvh` without page scrolling.
- Soft ivory, blush, peach, rose and pale-lavender palette.
- Three balanced milestone cards for August 05, 06 and 07.
- Floating circular date medallions with completed and locked states.
- Animated dotted journey path and heart markers.
- Live countdown with animated number changes.
- Falling petals, glowing sparkles and subtle background movement.
- Compact layouts for shorter screens and narrow devices.
- Reduced-motion accessibility support.

The updated component is in `src/App.jsx`, and its final styles are appended to `src/styles.css` under **THREE LITTLE DAYS V3**.
