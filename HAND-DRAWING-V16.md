# Deepest Wish – Hand Drawing V16

The Deepest Wish scene now uses a clean animated line-art illustration instead of pseudo-realistic or filled cartoon hands.

The animation sequence is:
1. outer heart / arms outline draws in,
2. the hand clasp lines are drawn,
3. the contact heart appears,
4. the small heart continues a gentle pulse,
5. the caption `till the very end` fades in.

No generated raster hand image is required for this illustration.
