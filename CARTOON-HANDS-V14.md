# Deepest Wish – Cartoon Holding Hands V14

The hand artwork on the Deepest Wish scene is now rendered entirely in code using SVG and Framer Motion.

Design direction:
- soft romantic cartoon/vector style
- clean readable hand silhouettes
- two distinct hands meeting naturally at the center
- gentle slide-in and squeeze motion
- pulsing glowing heart at the touch point
- subtle sparkles and soft shadows
- responsive on desktop and mobile

No external hand illustration asset is used for this scene.
