# Birthday Constellation Finale

The final memory section has been redesigned as a twelve-star birthday constellation.

## Add the final 12 photos

Place the images in:

`public/finale-photos/`

Use:

- `finale-01.jpg`
- `finale-02.jpg`
- `finale-03.jpg`
- `finale-04.jpg`
- `finale-05.jpg`
- `finale-06.jpg`
- `finale-07.jpg`
- `finale-08.jpg`
- `finale-09.jpg`
- `finale-10.jpg`
- `finale-11.jpg`
- `finale-12.jpg`

JPEG, PNG and WebP are detected automatically.

## Finale flow

1. A constellation star wakes up.
2. Its photo travels to the centre.
3. A personal birthday wish appears beside the photo.
4. The heart outline and butterflies complete the memory.
5. After all twelve memories, the constellation reveals the final heartfelt birthday wish.

## Photo timing

Each photo remains for approximately 6.8 seconds. Clicking a constellation star pauses autoplay and opens that memory.
