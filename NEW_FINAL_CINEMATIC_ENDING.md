# New Candle-to-Finale Ending

After **Blow the candle**:

1. The page becomes a plain deep-black and antique-gold cinematic screen.
2. `Once again, Happy Birthday to you, Chellom.` is written in the centre.
3. The writing fades away.
4. All 12 finale photos fly toward the screen together and form a heart-shaped collage.
5. `finale-12` becomes the special centre portrait.
6. The centre portrait displays `For my princess` and the final birthday wish.

## Finale photo files

Place the final photos inside:

`public/finale-photos/`

Use:

- finale-01.jpg
- finale-02.jpg
- ...
- finale-12.jpg

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP are supported automatically.

`finale-12` is used as the special centre photo. To use another photo as the centre, reorder the `finalePhotos` array in `src/data/memories.js`.
