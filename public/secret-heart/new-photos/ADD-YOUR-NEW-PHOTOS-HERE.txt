SECRET HEART — NEW PHOTO FOLDER
===============================

Put NEW photos only in this folder.
The Secret Heart pages no longer use the old /public/photos images as fallbacks.

Use these exact BASE filenames. Any of these extensions work:
.jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp

1. agnes-main
   Main Agnes portrait used on the "Every name still leads back to you" page.
   Best ratio: portrait 4:5 or 3:4.

2. memory-01
3. memory-02
4. memory-03
5. memory-04
6. memory-05
7. memory-06
   Six completely NEW photos for the Secret Heart memories page.
   Best ratio: portrait or square; the layout crops automatically.

8. proposal-main
   Main photo on the final question/proposal page.
   Best ratio: portrait 4:5.

9. proposal-small
   Small secondary memory shown beside the proposal photo.
   Best ratio: portrait/square.

10. keepsake
    Photo shown after the YES flow on the keepsake/final chapter page.
    Best ratio: portrait 4:5.

Example:
  agnes-main.jpg
  memory-01.png
  memory-02.webp
  memory-03.jpg
  memory-04.jpeg
  memory-05.jpg
  memory-06.png
  proposal-main.jpg
  proposal-small.webp
  keepsake.jpg

IMPORTANT
---------
Do not put these new Secret Heart images in /public/photos.
Keep them here so the public birthday/gallery photos and private Secret Heart photos stay separate.

If a photo is missing, the page shows a clean "Add new Secret Heart photo" placeholder instead of using an old photo.
