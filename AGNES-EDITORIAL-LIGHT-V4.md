# Agnes Scene V4 — Airy Editorial Light

This redesign replaces the previous framed/arched portrait treatment with a clean editorial layout.

## What changed
- Removed the arch frame, 3D stacked layers, orbit rings, rotated side label, and giant letter watermark from the Agnes scene.
- Moved the story copy to the left and the portrait to the right for a completely different composition.
- Portrait is now a large clean image panel with only a subtle soft shadow and light gradient treatment.
- Added two small floating word pills and a minimal heart accent.
- Changed the "A Little Truth" area into a simple editorial accent rather than another card.
- Refined light cream/blush palette and responsive mobile layout.

All Secret Heart flow, password, scene navigation, music and later pages remain unchanged.
