# Three Little Days — Light Storybook Redesign

The three-day surprise section now uses a soft, premium light-colour design.

## Design direction
- Warm ivory, blush pink, peach and pale lavender palette
- Asymmetrical editorial layout instead of three equal cards
- August 5 shown as the main open chapter
- August 6 and 7 shown as a clean vertical story timeline
- Live countdowns remain active
- Completed days automatically change to an opened state
- Soft floating petals, pastel glow, floral detail and gentle motion
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile

No photos or other website sections were changed.
