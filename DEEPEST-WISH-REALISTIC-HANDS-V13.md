# Deepest Wish – V13

The hand-holding visual in the Deepest Wish scene is now rendered directly with animated SVG code rather than a static/generated hand image.

The sequence is:
1. Left and right hands move toward one another.
2. Detailed hand contour strokes draw across the illustration.
3. The hands settle into the holding pose.
4. A heart appears at the meeting point and begins a soft pulse.
5. Small light particles appear around the heart.
6. The final line fades in beneath the illustration.
